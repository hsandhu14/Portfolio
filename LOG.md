## Entry 1: Starting from the assignment

**What I asked:** I gave the AI the full Artifact 1 assignment text and asked it to help me build the portfolio site: an intro, five artifact cards (card 1 real, cards 2 to 5 placeholders), and a log.

**What came back:** A simple single-page site with an intro section, five cards with title, link, and reflection areas, and a template for this log file. It used plain CSS with a light and dark theme based on the device setting.

**What I changed / what it misread:** I preferred a different look, so I built my own version (`portfolio_temp.html`) with a Tailwind dark-mode design: a sticky header with a theme toggle, a hero section, and a card grid where Artifact 1 is highlighted as "Active" and 2 to 5 are dimmed as "Upcoming". I used mine instead of the AI's first version. The AI's version also had no space for a LOG.md link or repository link, which mine includes.

## Entry 2: Adding my name

**What I asked:** I shared my page and asked the AI to add my name, Harman Sandhu, to it.

**What came back:** My name added in the browser tab title, the header brand (logo changed from "CS" to "HS"), the hero badge, and the footer. Everything else in my design stayed the same.

**What I changed / what it misread:** Nothing needed fixing. The AI also pointed out what the page was still missing for the assignment: an About section, a reflection on the Artifact 1 card, real ideas on cards 2 to 5, working links, and this log file.

## Entry 3: About Me section

**What I asked:** I told the AI I'm a senior at UW Tacoma, that I enjoy coding, and that my goal is to build five nice artifacts I can use on my resume.

**What came back:** A new "About Me" section between the hero and the artifacts grid, matching the rest of the design and using only what I told it.

**What I changed / what it misread:** Nothing needed fixing. I kept the text short and may add my interests later.

## Entry 4: Links and deployment

**What I asked:** I gave the AI my GitHub username (`hsandhu14`) and repo name (`Portfolio`) and asked it to fill in the links.

**What came back:** All five placeholder `#` links were replaced: the two LOG.md buttons point to `LOG.md` in the repo, and the three repository buttons point to the repo itself. They open in a new tab.

**What I changed / what it misread:** The AI could not test the links in a browser, so I need to click each one after deploying to confirm they work.

**Deployment steps:**
1. Uploaded `index.html` and `LOG.md` to the root of the `Portfolio` repo.
2. Turned on GitHub Pages (Settings > Pages, deploy from the `main` branch, root folder).
3. Opened the live site and tested the buttons and the theme toggle.
